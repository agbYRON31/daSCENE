WEALTH DESK 2.0 — READ ME FIRST

What it is
  An independent financial education and illustration app: 109 calculators, a
  five-question Client Mode, live Treasury yields and the RoRo Composite market
  dashboard. It is NOT a Morgan Stanley product and has not been reviewed or
  approved by Morgan Stanley or any other firm.

Install
  1. Drag "Wealth Desk" onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications. Requires macOS 13 or later (Apple silicon or Intel).

If macOS says it "cannot be opened" or "cannot verify the developer"
  This copy is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or notarized by Apple, so
  macOS Gatekeeper warns about it. That warning is expected for this build.
  - On a personal Mac you may approve it yourself in
    System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".
  - On a firm-managed Mac, do not override the warning. Ask your IT or
    compliance team; firm policy may not allow unapproved software.

Privacy
  - No account, sign-in, analytics or cloud storage.
  - Numbers typed into calculators are kept in memory only and erased on quit.
    They are never sent anywhere. A scenario file or PDF is written only when
    you choose Save, to the place you choose.
  - The app downloads public market and economic data from fred.stlouisfed.org,
    home.treasury.gov, query1.finance.yahoo.com and www.irs.gov. Those requests
    contain series names and ticker symbols only.

Important
  Educational calculations and hypothetical illustrations only. Not investment,
  tax or legal advice, and not a recommendation. Do not enter real client
  information unless your firm has approved this tool for that purpose.
